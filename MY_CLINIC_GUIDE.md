# VetPilot: My Clinic and sharing

My Clinic replaces the X-Ray tab. Create protocols or reusable equipment sets, add ordered steps, equipment quantities, sizes and storage locations, and attach reference photos and notes. Search includes steps and equipment. Favorites and recent items are saved on this device. Checklist checkmarks are temporary and are not exported.

## Send a copy

Open an item and scroll to Share / export, or use the sharing actions on the My Clinic list to export all items.

- **Email PDF / Email editable file** creates an Apple Mail draft with an attachment. Review recipients and tap Send. Configure an account in Apple's Mail app first; VetPilot never asks for its password.
- **Text PDF / Text editable file** creates a Messages draft with an attachment. Review the recipient and tap Send. File attachments require a supported messaging service; plain SMS cannot carry files, and attachment size limits apply.
- **Share PDF / Share editable file** opens the system share sheet for installed apps, AirDrop or Save to Files. Choosing Save to Files lets you select the destination. Exports are not automatically placed in Downloads.

The settings gear opens Sharing Settings. Optional email and phone defaults are recipient addresses, not sending-account logins. Apple Mail and Messages manage sending accounts. Third-party sharing apps manage their own recipients and accounts.

A PDF is for reading. An editable `.vetpilot` file preserves the protocol, steps, equipment, notes, photos and sender-entered review details. Open it in VetPilot or choose Import in My Clinic, review the preview, then choose Import copies. Existing items are never overwritten. Sharing is a copy, not live synchronization.

User-created and imported clinical content needs review before use. Reviewer/date fields are attribution supplied by the user, not a verified signature. Content changes clear unchanged prior review details.

## Medication screen

Choose the weight, formulation, dose level, frequency and available supply duration before Calculate. The calculated candidate and quantity appear first; the equations, math and source information follow below. Existing source rules, blocked entries and dosing safeguards remain in force. Software checks do not replace clinical review.

## Device checks

Before relying on sharing, use an iPhone configured for Mail and Messages to send a sample PDF and editable file to yourself, confirm both attachments arrive, and import the editable copy. Simulator tests can check file generation, stored preferences and unavailable-service fallbacks, but cannot establish email or carrier delivery.
